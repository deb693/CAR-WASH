Before we begin.

Development terms — service scope, cancellation rules and final commercial details must be approved before launch.

This local version is for testing. Its sample prices, coverage and package inclusions are not a live offer.

Requests and appointments

Submitting a booking request does not confirm an appointment. An appointment is confirmed only after explicit business confirmation. An enquiry does not reserve a time.

Packages and extras

The review step shows the server-calculated quote, selected extras and estimated duration. An expired or changed quote must be refreshed and reviewed before submission.

Payment

No online payment is collected through this website. A booking request records a preference to pay after the service.

Access and suitability

Provide relevant location and parking details so the team can assess the request. Vehicle condition, access restrictions and final service suitability may require discussion.

Changes and cancellation

Contact the business with your reference to discuss changes. The business must supply and approve cancellation periods, fees, exclusions and other applicable terms before launch.
